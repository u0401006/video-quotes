10 句金言
01 / 10
「
寫程式的成本越來越低，
反而讓「決定寫什麼」這件事
變得越來越珍貴。
"As code becomes much cheaper to write, the thing that becomes more valuable is deciding what to write."
CW
Cat Wu Head of Product, Claude Code · Anthropic
02 / 10
「
職稱是假的。
理解限制條件，搞清楚能做什麼，
然後快速嘗試、從錯誤中學習，
做錯了就道歉或修正。
"Jobs are fake. If you understand the constraints, you can figure out what you can do and then just try to do it quickly, learn from the mistakes, and apologize or fix them if you did something wrong."
CW
Cat Wu Head of Product, Claude Code · Anthropic
03 / 10
「
為超強 AGI 打造產品很容易。
難的是：在現有模型上，
如何把它的能力逼到極限。
"It is very easy to build the product for a super AGI strong model. The hard thing is figuring out for the current model how do you elicit the maximum capability."
CW
Cat Wu Head of Product, Claude Code · Anthropic
04 / 10
「
所有角色正在融合。
PM 在寫程式、工程師在做 PM、
設計師在制定策略，同時也在提交程式碼。
"All of the roles are merging. PMs are doing some engineering work, engineers are doing PM work, designers are PMing and also landing code."
CW
Cat Wu Head of Product, Claude Code · Anthropic
05 / 10
「
以前上線有問題的功能，我會輾轉難眠。
現在我能接受了——
因為我知道下一版就會修好。
"Launching a feature that is buggy is the kind of thing that would have kept me up at night. But I am now able to live with it knowing we'll fix it in the next release."
CW
Cat Wu Head of Product, Claude Code · Anthropic
06 / 10
「
打造 AI 原生產品，最重要的是：
快速迭代——
找到方法，讓你每週都能上線功能。
"The thing that is extremely important for building AI native products is iterating so quickly, figuring out a way for you to actually launch features every single week."
CW
Cat Wu Head of Product, Claude Code · Anthropic
07 / 10
「
你圍繞著某個限制設計的產品，
可能在開發到一半時，
那個限制就消失了。
"Exponentially improving models break that assumption. The constraints you designed around might disappear mid-project."
CW
Cat Wu Head of Product, Claude Code · Anthropic
08 / 10
「
新的產品管理節奏：
快速實驗、持續上線、
把有效的事情加倍放大。
"The new product management rhythm is rapid experimentation, consistent shipping, and doubling down on what works."
CW
Cat Wu Head of Product, Claude Code · Anthropic
09 / 10
「
當 PM 能在一個下午從想法變成原型，
「要不要試試看…」
與「來，試試這個」的距離幾乎消失。
"When a product manager can go from idea to working prototype in an afternoon, the gap between 'what if we tried…' and 'here, try this' nearly disappears."
CW
Cat Wu Head of Product, Claude Code · Anthropic
10 / 10
「
不需要做上百個評測才有用。
只要建立十個好的評測，
就能幫助團隊量化目標，以及衡量進展。
"You do not need to build hundreds of evals for them to be useful. Just building ten great evals is important for helping the team quantify what the goal is and what their progress towards it is."
CW
